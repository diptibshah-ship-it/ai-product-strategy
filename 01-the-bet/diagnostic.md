# Three-Axis Vulnerability Diagnostic

## Product
**Product:** Netflix  
**Your Role:** Product Manager

---

## Scores

### Contextual Moat — 3/5
*Workflow depth × switching cost. Would users leave in a weekend if a competitor showed up?*

**Score rationale:**
Netflix is deeply embedded in daily entertainment routines across devices (profiles, continue-watching, personalized queues, downloads, parental controls). Those integrations and curated watchlists create moderate switching cost. However subscriptions are month-to-month, many competitors hold exclusive content, and consumers frequently trial or rotate services, so outright churn is common when content shifts or price perception changes.

**Named attacker (from partner challenge):**
Disney+ / Amazon Prime Video / HBO Max (content-driven pull); telcos or cable bundles that include competing services.

---

### Data Advantage — 5/5
*Proprietary signal that compounds with usage. What do you see that OpenAI doesn't?*

**Score rationale:**
Netflix has one of the largest, high‑quality behavioral datasets for long‑form video: per‑second attention, completions, rewatches, A/B testing at scale, viewing sequences, and engagement linked to content metadata and production outcomes. That signal directly informs content commissioning, personalization, and UI experiments — hard for a new entrant to reproduce quickly.

**Named attacker (from partner challenge):**
Amazon (Prime has commerce + viewing signals) and large platforms (Google/Apple) if they gain equivalent long-form viewing telemetry via distribution partnerships.

---

### Platform Exposure — 3/5
*Encroachment risk × pivot speed. If Apple/Google/OpenAI ships your hero feature native — then what?*

**Score rationale:**
Device/platform owners (Apple, Google, Amazon, Roku, Samsung) can influence discoverability (home screen, remote prominence), bundle services, or add OS-level discovery features that reduce Netflix’s surface advantage. They cannot instantly replicate Netflix’s original content library or decades of viewing data, but they can erode distribution/visibility or bundle competing content, and can add discovery features that reduce the marginal benefit of Netflix’s recommendation UX.

**Named attacker (from partner challenge):**
Apple (bundling and home/TV UX control), Roku/Amazon (home screen/partner promotions), Comcast/telcos that can bundle alternatives.

---

## Top Vulnerability
Studios and platform owners pulling rights and aggressively bundling/producing exclusive content — combined with device/platform control over discovery — can erode Netflix’s content advantage and reduce subscriber retention while raising content acquisition costs.
